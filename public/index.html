Asked often. Answered plainly.

Is the testnet really private?

Yes. Every transfer on the running testnet uses a real ring of sixteen, real one-time keys and real commitments with range proofs, and payments to selenq1 addresses carry real ML-KEM-768 capsules. The explorer has no sender, receiver or amount to show you. Each transaction page has a button that re-runs the ring signatures, range proofs and balance check inside your own browser.

What exactly is quantum-proof?

The part that would leak backwards in time: who received what. Older private chains find receivers with elliptic-curve Diffie–Hellman, which Shor's algorithm breaks. SELEN quantum addresses carry an ML-KEM-768 public key, the lattice KEM NIST standardised as FIPS 203. A fresh secret is encapsulated for each payment and the 1,088-byte capsule travels with the output. Only the receiver can open it, quantum computer or not.

Why still use ring signatures?

They are fast, thoroughly studied, and no machine that breaks them exists today. If one appears, the most it can learn from a recorded ring is which of the sixteen members was really spent, not who received the money or how much. Receivers and amounts are what leak forever, so those are sealed first. Perigee replaces the ring with hash-based spend proofs.

Where are my keys?

Only in your browser. The wallet creates a 32-byte seed on your device, shows it as 24 words, encrypts it with your password and derives the spend key, view key and ML-KEM key from it. The node never sees any of them. Write the 24 words down: they are the only way back in.

How do I mine?

Open /mine, paste a SELEN address or use your wallet's, and press start. Your browser computes Argon2id proof of work in background threads, and every block you find pays its reward to that address as a private output. For more hashrate, run the command-line miner against this node or your own.

What is the difference between tSELEN and $SELEN?

tSELEN is the testnet coin from the faucet and from mining. It has no value and will not carry over. $SELEN is the Robinhood Chain token that funds development and carries the project to mainnet.

Can I run my own node?

Yes. Node, explorer and wallet are a single open-source Node.js program. Run npm install and npm start, and set PEER= to a public node to download and re-verify the entire chain yourself.

When is mainnet?

After Perigee is written up, tested and reviewed in public. Mainnet starts from a new genesis with Perigee switched on at block one.
